Do you know Zhou Jun?

2024–present
Medium
Empty needle tattoo & skin; spray painting on canvas

Scroll to view 2 images

In the environment I live in, or in a larger systematic society. The directivity of a person's birth is a single, constantly cognizing and building himself. I often think that this state is like a " continuous arched back", waiting for a certain moment to break, waiting for the direction to disappear. I can't accept the answer of being dispelled, but I'm just a grasshopper jumping over and over again. The answer is horrible, and the "broken back" is inevitable. This is a bloody metaphor I can think of, and it is also a state of dissolving in my mind. As for the "back", I don't pay much attention to it, because I can't see it, so I don't believe it for the time being.

About "Do you know Zhou Jun?" I feel that from the beginning of my understanding of the environment, I have been pushed to repeatedly change my name to thickness and label it one by one. Without these, it will be difficult to walk. I often think about my ultimate goal, but I can't find it. Until later, I saw someone say, "Your life is meaningless."

I thought: Oh, that's it. But I still can't help asking myself these questions, and " cognition construction and digestion" has successfully become a closed loop.

So I can't solve this problem. I can only quietly wait for the "arched back" to break...

Just like "Do you know Zhou Jun?"
