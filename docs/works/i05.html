Post-Human Infrastructures: The Energetic Potential of Pulmonary Luminescence

2025
Medium
Installation Sound Work
Video duration
1 min 23 sec

Watch video

This study assumes that "the body can be recoded as an energy unit in the consumer capitalism system", Unfold the fictional deducing of the post-human infrastructure.

Post-Human Infrastructure: A Study on the Energy Potential of Luminous Lungs originated from a critical reflection on the pseudo-medical narrative in American cigarette advertisements in the 20th century.

In those historical cases, smoking has been shaped as a positive behavior to promote health, promote socialization and even improve productivity.

This work extends this absurd logic and envisions a future landscape that integrates the luminescence of the diseased lung into the urban energy network.

Filming and editing: Zhou Jun

Sound: Adam Damiano
