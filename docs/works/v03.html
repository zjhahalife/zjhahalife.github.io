EGG.PRISON.MOVEMENT.ID PHOTO

2023
Medium
PVC paper, mechanical ready-made products
Dimensions
15 × 10 cm

Scroll to view 2 images

Last year, on my plane trip to Hong Kong, I watched the movie " Us and Them" . Although the film has nothing to do with the installation I'm about to introduce, through one of the clips, I was able to find an object that I owned as a child that resembles a moving lamp in the underwater world. This discovery sparked a strong interest in it, leading me to look for a similar product online in vain. It wasn't until the beginning of the year that I finally found the item and decided to make some changes to it.

Photography: Haodong Ye
