Indeterminate Gait

2025
Medium
Welded metal, brass duck-head handle (found object), coat hook, fabric work coat, clay, plastic craft gems, spray paint
Dimensions
Irregular dimensions

Scroll to view 3 images

This work started with a brass duck head taken from an old cane. Its original function and its sense of etiquette were still present, but fragile. I wanted to give it a new body that no longer followed the logic of a single species or a single purpose.

The leg is welded from metal and merges a human foot structure with the form of a bird’s talon. It stays somewhere between anatomy and fabrication, like a limb that has passed through different owners or different biological rules.

The pink clay growths and the small plastic gems refer to two opposite impulses—childhood ornament and pathological swelling. They sit together without resolving the tension.

I added a hook behind the handle so it can hold my coat or work jacket. When clothing hangs from it, the object feels partly functional again, but in a way that doesn’t fully return to its past identity. It becomes something that stands there for me—half companion, half tool.

The work is less about creating a hybrid creature and more about loosening the categories that decide what a body or an object should be. It tries to keep the moment when an identity is still shifting, when posture has not yet settled into a final form.
